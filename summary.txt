Wednesday, 11 November 2026. In Abu Dhabi, two days of US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine ended with a deal on a large prisoner exchange but no progress on territory or a ceasefire, Reuters and AP reported. Envoy Jared Kushner called it the biggest humanitarian release yet, with numbers to be set Thursday; Russia's Vladimir Medinsky and Ukraine's Rustem Umerov confirmed it. Zelenskyy, who skipped the talks, had floated 1,000 to 1,500 prisoners a side. Kushner and Steve Witkoff meet Putin and Zelenskyy separately on Thursday.

On Iran, Marco Rubio told G7 ministers in Paris that US sanctions and a naval blockade were working well enough that there was "no need" for major combat operations, the New York Times reported, though force stayed an option if Iran crossed red lines. Security chief Mohsen Rezaei said no enriched material would leave Iran and any new US strike would be answered by attacks on distant US bases, Reuters reported. Tehran also listed Argentina's navy and air force as terrorist organisations, answering Argentina's April designation of the Revolutionary Guards. The US Justice Department charged three men it calls Iran-backed operatives over plots in the US and Europe, the latest at RAF Fairford; Iran denied any role, the BBC reported.

House Republicans blocked impeachment charges against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth by 211-204, with 11 Democrats absent; Trump called it an exoneration. They elected Jim Jordan as House leader, replacing Speaker Mike Johnson in January when a Democratic majority of at least 256 seats takes over, and reelected John Thune to lead Senate Republicans. Democrats hold 54 Senate seats, and Bernie Sanders said he will not back Chuck Schumer for majority leader.

Israeli fire killed at least seven people in Gaza, including a father and his 13-year-old son in Zeitoun, medics told Reuters. At COP31 in Turkey, held without the United States, UN chief Antonio Guterres said the climate crisis is outrunning the world.
